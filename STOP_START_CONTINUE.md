Stop / Start / Continue — Finalized (Modules 2–8)

Use this as the signed worksheet content to copy into your Stop/Start/Continue form and share with your supervisor for signature.

Module 2 — Leadership in the Moment
- STOP: Using reactive language ("if only", "I can't"); blaming circumstances for inaction.
- START: Daily proactive planning: 5–10 minute morning routine using MASTER (Measure, Assess, See, Think, Engage, Review) and a short "sharpen the saw" break each hour.
- CONTINUE: Practice renewal (Sharpen the Saw) across Physical, Mental, Social/Emotional, Spiritual dimensions.

Module 3 — Relationship Management
- STOP: Listening autobiographically (probing/evaluating/advising/interpreting before understanding).
- START: Practice empathic listening: ask "What can I do to help you in this situation?" and mirror key emotions before responding.
- CONTINUE: Keep commitments (small promises) to build trust; apply the 13 trust behaviors in daily interactions.

Module 4 — Delivering Great Customer Service
- STOP: Delivering only the functional brief; ignoring social and emotional customer jobs.
- START: Use the CPR escalation (Content → Pattern → Relationship) for recurring issues and run one deep-customer interview per week.
- CONTINUE: Lead with optimism and playfulness where appropriate to build rapport and improve engagement.

Module 5 — Creative Thinking
- STOP: Defaulting to standard procedures without challenging assumptions.
- START: Daily 10-minute creativity warm-up (puzzles / lateral-thinking prompts) and a weekly 30-minute idea-generation session (structured brainstorming).
- CONTINUE: Use deliberate ideation (planned, documented) practices and iterate on prototypes.

Module 6 — Becoming a Leader
- STOP: Equating title or role with leadership; avoiding development conversations.
- START: Weekly one-on-ones focused on development (strengths inventory + 1 improvement goal). Create a simple development plan for at least one direct report.
- CONTINUE: Model authenticity and likability behaviors (consistent tone, transparency, and presence).

Module 7 — Building a High-Performance Team
- STOP: Avoiding healthy conflict and letting decisions remain ambiguous.
- START: Use visible team scoreboards and end-of-meeting 1-line decisions (who, what, when) to force clarity and closure.
- CONTINUE: Encourage peer-to-peer accountability and recognize collective results publicly.

Module 8 — Coaching for Success
- STOP: Jumping to advice/solutions before exploring the coachee's perspective (feedback-as-first-response).
- START: Run at least one GROW coaching session per week with a team member (Goal, Reality, Options, Way forward) and document commitments.
- CONTINUE: Use the six-question approach and peer coaching practices to unlock potential — focus on future actions, not past blame.

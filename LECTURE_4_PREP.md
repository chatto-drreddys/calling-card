## Core Frameworks for Module 3 (Exam-Tested)

### Habit 5 — Seek First to Understand, Then to Be Understood
> **"Most people do not listen with the intent to understand; they listen with the intent to reply."** — Stephen Covey

**Why it matters:** You cannot lead, manage, or build relationships if you don't understand the other person first.

#### 4 Levels of Listening (low → high)
1. **Ignoring** — not listening at all
2. **Pretending** — "Yeah... uh huh..."
3. **Selective** — hearing only parts that interest you
4. **Attentive** — paying attention, focusing on words
5. **Empathic** — listening with intent to understand (feeling, meaning, not just words)

*Empathic = the goal. This is Habit 5.*

#### 4 Autobiographical Responses (why we fail to listen — exam trap)
When we listen to reply instead of to understand, we fall into these traps:
1. **Evaluate** — agree or disagree
2. **Probe** — ask from our own frame of reference
3. **Advise** — give counsel based on our own experience
4. **Interpret** — explain motives based on our own experience

*All 4 are self-referential. They make the conversation about YOU, not the other person.*

#### Empathic Listening in Practice
- Reflect feeling: "It sounds like you're frustrated that..."
- Reflect content: "So what you're saying is..."
- Both: "It sounds like you feel [emotion] because [reason]..."
- **Do NOT:** jump to solutions, compare to your own experience, or finish their sentences

### Stephen M.R. Covey — 13 Behaviors of Trust
*(From "The Speed of Trust" — this is exam-tested)*

**Trust = Character + Competence**
- **Character** = who you are (integrity, intent)
- **Competence** = what you do (capabilities, results)
- Both are required. High character, low competence = well-meaning but unreliable. High competence, low character = skilled but dangerous.

#### The 13 Behaviors (memorize all 13)

**Character-based (5):**
1. **Talk Straight** — be honest, tell the truth, use clear language
2. **Demonstrate Respect** — genuinely care for others
3. **Create Transparency** — be open, no hidden agendas
4. **Right Wrongs** — apologize quickly, make restitution
5. **Show Loyalty** — give credit to others, speak about people as if they're present

**Competence-based (5):**
6. **Deliver Results** — establish a track record of results
7. **Get Better** — continuously improve, learn from experience
8. **Confront Reality** — address the tough stuff directly
9. **Clarify Expectations** — create shared understanding, don't assume
10. **Practice Accountability** — hold yourself and others accountable

**Both Character + Competence (3):**
11. **Listen First** — listen before you speak; understand before prescribing
12. **Keep Commitments** — say what you're going to do, do what you say
13. **Extend Trust** — give trust abundantly to those who have earned it; conditionally to those who are earning it

---

### The Trust Tax and Trust Dividend
- **Low trust = Tax:** Everything costs more — more time, more communication, more bureaucracy, more politics
- **High trust = Dividend:** Everything moves faster — better collaboration, faster decisions, less re-work
- *This is why relationship management matters: trust is the fastest ROI in leadership*

Revalida Presentation — Slide Draft (25 minutes)

Use this deck to copy into PowerPoint or Google Slides. Each slide includes speaker notes (keep to timings).

Slide 1 — Title / Opening (1 minute)
- Title: "LEAD Revalida — [Your Name]"
- Subtitle: Calling Card (top 1 preference + chosen environment)
- Speaker note: 30s personal intro, 30s calling: "My calling is [X]. I thrive in [environment]."

Slide 2 — Overview (30s)
- Bullet: Agenda: M2 → M8 highlights (stop/start/continue) + closing
- Speaker note: Set expectation: 25 minutes + Q&A

Slide 3 — Module 2: Leadership in the Moment (2.5 min)
- STOP: Reactive language / blaming
- START: Daily proactive planning (MASTER) + hourly "sharpen the saw" break
- CONTINUE: Renewal across physical, mental, social, spiritual
- Speaker note: Briefly describe one example where you applied MASTER this week and the result (30–45s). Then state the personal commitment (15s).

Slide 4 — Module 3: Relationship Management (2.5 min)
- STOP: Listening autobiographically
- START: Empathic listening: ask "What can I do to help you in this situation?"
- CONTINUE: Keep commitments; practice 13 trust behaviors
- Speaker note: Describe a quick 1-minute example where empathic listening changed an outcome.

Slide 5 — Module 4: Delivering Great Customer Service (2.5 min)
- STOP: Delivering only functional specs
- START: Apply CPR escalation & one deep-customer interview per week
- CONTINUE: Lead with optimism/playfulness
- Speaker note: Share one customer interaction you'll change with CPR (30–40s) and a measurable outcome you expect.

Slide 6 — Module 5: Creative Thinking (2.5 min)
- STOP: Defaulting to procedures without challenge
- START: Daily 10-minute creativity warm-ups + weekly ideation sessions
- CONTINUE: Deliberate ideation & prototyping cycles
- Speaker note: Show a quick puzzle result or a 2-line example of an idea you generated during a warm-up and how you'll pilot it.

Slide 7 — Module 6: Becoming a Leader (2.5 min)
- STOP: Equating title with leadership
- START: Weekly one-on-ones; create a 1-page development plan for a direct report
- CONTINUE: Authenticity and consistent presence
- Speaker note: State one development goal for a team member and your planned check-ins (timeline).

Slide 8 — Module 7: Building a High-Performance Team (2.5 min)
- STOP: Avoiding healthy conflict
- START: Visible team scoreboards + end-of-meeting decisions (Who/What/When)
- CONTINUE: Peer accountability + public recognition of team results
- Speaker note: Explain what the scoreboard will track, who owns it, and the expected cadence.

Slide 9 — Module 8: Coaching for Success (2.5 min)
- STOP: Jumping to advice before listening
- START: Weekly GROW coaching sessions with one team member; document commitments
- CONTINUE: Six-question approach & peer coaching
- Speaker note: Run through a 30-second GROW micro-example: Goal → Reality → Options → Way forward.

Slide 10 — Summary & Closing (1.5 min)
- One-paragraph: "Who I am becoming as a leader" linking calling + top commitments across modules
- Ask: Request for feedback areas (2 specific asks from panel)
- Speaker note: Thank panel; ask for one area to improve after feedback.

Appendix slides (optional, keep as backup)
- Detailed Stop/Start/Continue per module (copy from STOP_START_CONTINUE.md)
- Supervisor signature checklist and planned dates

Conversion notes
- To convert this Markdown to PowerPoint with Pandoc:

```bash
pandoc REVALIDA_SLIDES.md -t pptx -o Revalida_Presentation.pptx --slide-level=2
```

- Or copy slide headings into Google Slides and paste speaker notes into the notes sections.

Design tips (quick):
- Use large sans-serif fonts (32–44 for headings, 20–28 for bullets)
- One idea per line, no more than 3 bullets per slide
- Use 1–2 images/icons to support your story (not decorative)
- Keep deck under 12 slides (we used 10 + appendix)
